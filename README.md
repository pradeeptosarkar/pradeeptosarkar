### 🔐 What I do

- **Security operations.** I work as a SecOps Analyst at IHG Hotels &amp; Resorts. Day to day that means monitoring, alert triage, investigations and incident response, plus the scripting and automation that make all of it faster.
- **NAMESPACE.** I am building [NAMESPACE](https://www.namespacecomm.in), an infrastructure layer for hackathons and open innovation that connects builders, communities and organizations. Event hosting, organization pages, verifiable credentials and the tooling that lets communities run their own hackathons with as little manual work as possible.
- **HACKHAZARDS.** NAMESPACE's flagship event and the World's Largest Community-run Hackathon.
